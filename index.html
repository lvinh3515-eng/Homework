Welcome to RimWorld!

Ever wanted to build a thriving colony, keep your people happy, and survive on a distant planet? Well, you've come to the right place! RimWorld is a sci-fi colony simulation game where every story is different. You'll manage resources, build shelters, research technology, defend against raiders, and try to keep your colonists alive. Sounds simple, right? Until someone starts a social fight, your food supply runs out, and a pack of angry animals decides to ruin your entire week. Whether you're a complete beginner or a veteran with hundreds of hours in the game, there's always something new to learn. So, grab your tools, prepare for the unexpected, and remember: everything is under control. Probably.
