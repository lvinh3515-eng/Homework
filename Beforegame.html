Before your first colony

Before you start your first colony, there are a few things you should know. RimWorld is a complex game with many mechanics, and understanding them will help you have a better experience. Here are some tips to get you started:

Choose your scenario wisely: RimWorld offers different scenarios that can affect your starting conditions. Some scenarios are easier than others, so choose one that matches your skill level.

My recommendation is crash lander as it basic and can help you get in to the game without too much difficulty. You can also try out other scenarios once you are more comfortable with the game mechanics.

Choose your storyteller: RimWorld has three storytellers that control the events in your game. Each storyteller has a different style and difficulty level. Choose one that matches your preferred playstyle.

My recommendation is phoebe chillax as she is the easiest and will give you more time to build your colony without too many interruptions. other are Cassandra classic and Randy random. Cassandra classic is a bit more challenging and will throw more events at you, while randy random is the most unpredictable and can be very difficult to manage.

Choose your landing site carefully: The landing site you choose will affect the resources available to you, the climate, and the types of threats you will face. Consider the pros and cons of each location before making your decision.

My recommendation is temperate forest as it is the easiest and will give you a good balance of resources and challenges. Other options include desert, tundra, and jungle, each with their own unique challenges and advantages.
